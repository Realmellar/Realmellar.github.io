Payments, Refunds and Creator Payouts

Company: VIBERA INTERACTIVE SYSTEMS LIMITED

Registration: RC 9943002, Nigeria

Status: Preliminary policy for review before launch.

VIBERA is under development. This page does not mean that payments, subscriptions, virtual coins, LIVE gifts or creator withdrawals are currently available. Final prices, payment providers, refund rules and payout terms must be confirmed before any paid service launches.

1. Planned paid services

VIBERA may introduce subscriptions, virtual items, LIVE gifts, advertising and music promotion. Each service must display its price, currency, payment frequency where applicable, and important conditions before a customer confirms a transaction.

No service should be advertised as available for purchase until its payment and fulfilment processes have been implemented and tested.

2. Prices and currencies

Prices and accepted currencies will be shown at checkout before payment. Any taxes, exchange-rate effects or applicable fees must be disclosed where required. A displayed local-currency estimate may differ from the final amount charged by a payment provider or bank.

3. Virtual coins and gifts

If virtual coins or LIVE gifts launch, the purchase screen must explain the applicable price, use restrictions and whether items expire or can be refunded. Virtual coins and gifts are not automatically cash, bank deposits or withdrawable balances.

Any conversion rate between coins and money must be explicitly published before purchases begin. No conversion rate is being promised by this preliminary page.

4. Creator earnings and payouts

VIBERA does not promise earnings simply for uploading videos or receiving views, likes or followers. Any future creator earnings programme will have separate eligibility, verification and payment conditions.

If LIVE gift revenue sharing launches, VIBERA's intended model is a 50% creator share and a 50% platform share of the defined eligible gift revenue. The final rules must explain how eligible revenue is calculated, including any taxes, refunds, chargebacks or provider fees that affect the calculation.

Creator withdrawals are planned to require eligibility checks and approval. Actual withdrawal availability, minimum amounts, processing times, supported countries, currencies and payment providers must be confirmed and published before the feature launches. No payout is guaranteed merely because an account displays a balance.

5. Refunds and cancellations

Before a paid service launches, VIBERA must publish a final refund and cancellation procedure explaining which purchases qualify, any applicable deadlines, how to submit a request, and expected processing times.

Until those details are confirmed, this preliminary page does not promise that a purchase is refundable or non-refundable. Mandatory consumer rights under applicable law will apply.

6. Failed payments and disputes

If a payment appears unsuccessful but money has been deducted, customers should first check their payment provider or bank's transaction status. Once an official support channel is published, customers will be able to use it to report unresolved payment issues. Duplicate charges, chargebacks and refunds will need a documented review process before paid services launch.

7. Payment security

VIBERA intends to use suitable payment providers and security safeguards. Payment-card details must be handled through an appropriately secured and compliant payment integration. This page does not claim that a payment integration or security certification is already in place.

8. Third-party payment providers

The providers used for collecting payments and paying eligible creators will be identified when selected and integrated. Provider eligibility, fees, verification requirements and country availability must be checked before a service is offered.

9. Fraud and account review

Transactions may require review to address suspected fraud, account compromise, legal obligations or payment disputes. The final policy will explain applicable restrictions, review procedures and any available appeal process.

11. Policy changes

These preliminary terms will be reviewed and updated before launch. Any final policy must accurately describe the payment services actually offered and comply with applicable laws and provider rules.
