2. Information we may collect

Depending on the services eventually launched, information may include:

  • Account details such as a username, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • Profile information, photos, videos, comments, messages and other content a user chooses to submit.
  • Technical information such as device type, app version, log data and security events.
  • Usage information needed to operate, troubleshoot and improve the service.
  • Payment or transaction records if paid services are introduced.
  • Reports, support requests and information users provide when contacting us.

The information actually collected will depend on the features used, the permissions granted and the final platform design. We will not claim to collect a category of information unless the relevant service does so.

3. How information may be used

Where applicable and lawful, information may be used to:

  • Create and secure accounts and provide requested services.
  • Display user-selected profiles and content to intended audiences.
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, spam and violations of platform rules.
  • Respond to support requests and user reports.
  • Process transactions and maintain financial records if payments launch.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve reliability and accessibility.
  • Meet legal obligations and protect users, the company and the public.

4. Legal grounds for processing

Where required by applicable law, we will identify an appropriate legal basis for processing personal data, such as providing a requested service, complying with a legal obligation, obtaining consent or pursuing a legitimate interest that does not override a person's rights.

5. Content and visibility

Social features may allow users to publish content to other people. Before publishing, users should review the available audience and privacy controls. Public content may be viewed, copied or shared by others. We will explain the actual visibility settings when those features are implemented.

6. Sharing information

Actual retention periods, security controls and deletion procedures must be documented before the corresponding services launch.
