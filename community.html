Community Guidelines

Company: VIBERA INTERACTIVE SYSTEMS LIMITED

Registration: RC 9943002, Nigeria

Status: Preliminary guidelines for review before launch.

VIBERA is under development. These guidelines describe the community standards the platform intends to apply. Reporting, moderation, appeals and enforcement systems must be implemented and tested before the platform is opened to the public.

1. Our community values

VIBERA aims to provide a welcoming place for people to share videos, communicate, discover entertainment and participate in communities. Every member is expected to treat others with dignity and respect.

2. Harassment and threats

Do not threaten, stalk, bully, intimidate or repeatedly target people with abusive conduct. Criticism and disagreement should not become targeted harassment or threats of violence.

3. Hate and discrimination

Content that promotes violence, dehumanization or targeted abuse against people because of protected or personal characteristics may be prohibited. The final policy will define enforcement standards and applicable exceptions.

4. Child safety

Sexual exploitation of children, child sexual abuse material, grooming and attempts to exploit or endanger minors are prohibited. Such content must not be uploaded, requested, shared or promoted. VIBERA intends to establish appropriate reporting and escalation procedures before launch.

5. Sexual and violent content

Content involving non-consensual intimate imagery, sexual exploitation, credible threats, graphic violence or encouragement of serious harm may be removed or restricted. Final age-related rules and content limits must be established before public access.

6. Scams, fraud and impersonation

Do not impersonate another person or organization, run deceptive schemes, manipulate users into sending money, or make fraudulent claims about investments, giveaways, earnings or platform services. Do not falsely claim to represent VIBERA.

7. Copyright and ownership

Share only content you created or have permission or another lawful basis to use. Do not distribute copyrighted music, movies, broadcasts or other material unlawfully. A future copyright complaint and counter-notice procedure must be published where required.

8. Spam and platform manipulation

Do not use bots, fake accounts, purchased engagement, repetitive unwanted messages or deceptive methods to manipulate views, likes, followers, rankings, gifts or other platform systems.

9. LIVE and real-time interactions

If LIVE features launch, hosts and participants must follow these guidelines and any additional LIVE rules. Hosts should not encourage threats, exploitation, dangerous acts, scams or unlawful broadcasts. Music, movies and other third-party material must be used with the necessary rights and permissions.

10. Privacy and personal information

Do not publish another person's private information, login credentials, financial details or private images without lawful authority or consent. Do not use VIBERA to threaten someone with disclosure of private information.

11. Reporting concerns

VIBERA intends to provide reporting options for users to flag content, accounts and safety concerns. Until official reporting tools and a verified support channel are published, this page does not promise that an in-platform reporting system is available.

12. Review and enforcement

Depending on the seriousness and context of a violation, intended responses may include content restrictions, removal, warnings, temporary suspension or account termination. The final policy must explain review standards, appeals, urgent safety actions and any legally required escalation procedures.

13. Avoid retaliation

Do not threaten or harass people for making a good-faith report. Deliberately false reports and attempts to abuse reporting tools may themselves violate the final rules.

14. Updates

These guidelines will be reviewed and updated as VIBERA develops. The final version must reflect the actual features, safety systems and applicable legal requirements.
