Trust and responsibility

A safer digital community matters.

VIBERA intends to establish rules against harassment, scams, impersonation, unlawful content and violations of intellectual-property rights. Safety reporting, moderation and account controls must be implemented and tested before the relevant services launch.

Do not use a future VIBERA feature as though it is already available. Before launch, the company must provide working ways to report abuse, respond to complaints and enforce its published rules.
Business model

Transparent digital services.

Potential revenue areas include in-feed advertising, subscriptions, music promotion and voluntary LIVE gifts if those features launch. Any paid feature must clearly disclose its price and applicable terms before a customer pays.

Creator earnings, coin conversion rates, fees, eligibility, refunds and withdrawals are not offered by this website. They will require separate published terms and functioning payment systems before use. No earnings are guaranteed.
